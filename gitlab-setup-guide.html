# Contributing to Skills2Careers Compass

This project is owned by UNESCO and maintained under Apache License 2.0.

## Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA)

Before any contribution can be merged, you must sign the UNESCO CLA.
By submitting a merge request you confirm that:

1. You have the legal right to grant the rights described here.
2. Your contribution is your original work, or you have permission to submit it.
3. You assign to UNESCO all copyright in your contribution, granting UNESCO a
   perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use, copy, modify,
   distribute, and sublicense your contribution under any open-source licence
   UNESCO chooses to apply now or in the future.
4. You retain the right to use your contribution in other projects under any
   licence of your own choosing, provided that use does not represent a claim
   of ownership over the Skills2Careers Compass codebase.

To sign the CLA, open a GitLab issue titled "CLA: [Your Name]" and include:

  "I, [Full Name], agree to the UNESCO Skills2Careers Compass Contributor
   Licence Agreement as described in CONTRIBUTING.md. I confirm that all
   contributions I submit are my original work and that I have the right
   to assign copyright to UNESCO."

A UNESCO team member will countersign and close the issue.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository under your own GitLab namespace.
2. Create a feature branch: git checkout -b feature/my-change
3. Make your changes, keeping commits focused and descriptive.
4. Open a Merge Request against the main branch.
5. Reference your CLA issue in the MR description.

## Code standards

- No build step required. The application runs as a static site.
- JavaScript: ES2020, no frameworks. Follow the existing module structure.
- Data files are JSON. Validate with: python validate_data.py
- Do not commit node_modules/, .netlify/, or any credentials or API keys.

## Governance

Major changes require Governance Board approval. See GOVERNANCE.md.

# Skills2Careers Compass — Governance

## Ownership

Skills2Careers Compass is owned by UNESCO. The canonical source of
record is: code.unesco.org/skills2careers/compass

## Governance Board

### Permanent seats (voting — cannot be removed or transferred)

| Seat              | Holder                                    | Role                         |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Global Custodian  | UNESCO HQ                                 | Platform owner; holds IP     |
| Regional Co-owner | Inter-University Council for East Africa  | EAC academic integration     |
| Regional Co-owner | East African Community Secretariat        | Regional policy alignment    |

### Observer seats (non-voting, Funder Advisory Panel)

Funders and implementing partners may hold observer seats.
Observer seats confer no governance rights and no veto.

## Decision-making

| Decision                              | Authority                              |
|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Day-to-day maintenance                | Maintainer team                        |
| New features                          | Maintainer team with UNESCO notification |
| New data source integrations          | Governance Board majority              |
| Architectural changes                 | Governance Board majority              |
| Changes to licence terms              | Governance Board unanimous vote        |
| Transfer of repository ownership      | Unanimous vote + UNESCO legal sign-off |
| Changes to permanent Board seats      | Unanimous vote + UNESCO legal sign-off |

## Licence-change veto

The licence may only be changed by unanimous vote of all three permanent
seat-holders. No funder, technology partner, or implementing organisation
may compel a licence change unilaterally. Any purported licence change
enacted without unanimous board approval is void.

## Forks and derivative works

Forks must:
- Retain the original copyright notice naming UNESCO as copyright holder.
- Include the NOTICE file unmodified (additional notices may be appended).
- Not use the UNESCO name, logo, or wordmark to imply UNESCO endorsement
  without separate written authorisation from UNESCO.

## Dispute resolution

Unresolved disputes are subject to the internal dispute resolution
mechanisms of UNESCO as an international organisation, in accordance
with the UNESCO Constitution and the 1947 Convention on the Privileges
and Immunities of the Specialized Agencies.

# Skills2Careers Compass

**© 2026 United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO)**

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache%202.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
[![UNESCO](https://img.shields.io/badge/Owner-UNESCO-e63329)](https://unesco.org)

A career navigation and labour market intelligence platform for East Africa,
supporting learners, educators, and policymakers across agriculture, energy,
and digital economy sectors. Developed in partnership with the Inter-University
Council for East Africa (IUCEA) and the East African Community (EAC).

---

## What it does

- **Career pathway explorer** — guided wizard matching learner profiles to
  sector-specific career pathways across three priority sectors and six EAC
  member states.
- **Labour market intelligence** — live and curated data on hot skills,
  occupations, wages, and employer demand, sourced from Lightcast, LinkedIn
  Economic Graph, and national LMIS.
- **Training supply observatory** — cross-sector view of course availability,
  provider landscape, regional access gaps, and skills coverage.
- **Resource hub** — curated scholarships, research reports, and sector evidence.

## Status

Prototype — validated with partner institutions. Production pathway in development.

## Technology

Vanilla JavaScript · Tailwind CSS · Chart.js · No build step required.
Deployable as a static site on Netlify, GitHub Pages, or any web server.

## Getting started

    git clone https://code.unesco.org/skills2careers/compass.git
    cd compass
    open index.html          # or: npx serve .
